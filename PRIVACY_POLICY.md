## 1. Overview
**IR Remote AC TV Freeware** is a 100% free and ad-free universal infrared remote application for Android. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and permissions.

## 2. No Personal Data Collection
We **do NOT collect, store, track, sell, or transmit** any personal data, usage analytics, or device identifiers. All functionality operates entirely offline on your device.

## 3. App Permissions & Their Purpose
The application requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function:

- **Infrared Emitter (`TRANSMIT_IR`):**  
  Used solely to transmit infrared light bursts to control your Air Conditioners, Televisions, and Set-Top Boxes via your device's built-in IR blaster.
- **Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`):**  
  Used exclusively for real-time optical scanning of remote configuration QR codes (CameraX viewfinder).  
  *No photos, video streams, or image data are ever stored, recorded, or transmitted to any server.*
- **In-App Purchases (`com.android.vending.BILLING`):**  
  Used solely to process voluntary, optional donations ("Cup of Tea") via the official Google Play In-App Billing API. All payment details are processed directly and securely by Google Play; the app has no access to your financial or card details.
- **Pinned Shortcuts (`INSTALL_SHORTCUT`):**  
  Used solely to place 1-tap launcher shortcuts on your phone's home screen for quick remote access.

## 4. Third-Party Services
This application does **NOT** use any third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising SDKs. The only external service is the Google Play Billing Library for optional voluntary donations handled by Google.

## 5. Children's Privacy
The app does not collect personal information from any individuals, including children under the age of 13.
